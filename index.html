About PDF Processor

A specialized tool for educators to stamp professional overlays onto student reports in bulk. Redact sensitive data, insert branding, or add feedback once and apply it to an entire class effortlessly.

Developed by Jonathan Kung

Workflow Guide

1

Reference Setup

Start in the Template Editor by uploading a single student report. This file acts as your positioning guide — your overlays will be placed relative to this document's layout.

2

Scale & Logic

Set your Pages Per Student accurately. This tells the processor exactly where one student's report ends and the next begins in your batch PDF.

3

Design Overlays

Use the toolbar to draw your overlays. Redact sensitive data, stamp custom Text labels, or place high-res Images (logos/stamps).

4

Visibility Control

Click any overlay to set its Visibility Logic. Choose between once per student, repeating on all pages, or specific page indices.

5

Save & Backup

Name and save your template. Use the Backup All feature to download a permanent copy of your library.

6

Run Processor

Switch to the Run tab, select your template, and drop your student batch. Review the live preview before downloading.

Common Questions

My overlays are appearing on the wrong pages. Why?
The most common cause is an incorrect Pages Per Student setting. Double-check that the count in your Template Editor matches the actual structure of your batch PDF.
My templates disappeared after clearing browser history.
Templates are stored in local storage. Always backup frequently using the "Backup All" feature in the sidebar to keep your templates safe.
Does this work for any PDF?
Yes. It works with any PDF. As long as each student's section has a consistent page count, the processor will handle it perfectly.

Managing Templates

Your library is organized for speed. From the Run tab, you can:

  • Favourite & Pin — Star your most used templates to keep them at the top. The top-pinned template is pre-selected on launch.
  • Batch Rename — Quickly rename templates without leaving the run view.
  • Deep Edit — Jump directly into the editor for any existing template to refine placements.
Advanced Processor Tips

Ghost Overlays

The editor displays faint "ghost" outlines on other pages to show where repeating elements will land. These are guides only and won't appear in the final PDF.

Dynamic Scaling

PDF Processor automatically calculates aspect ratios. Your overlays will match the real coordinates of each page, even if report sizes vary slightly.

Manual Save

Changes are cached but not finalized until you click Save. Use the "● Saved" indicator to verify your work is stored.

Storage Warning

Templates live in your browser's local cache. Clearing history or changing devices will erase your library.

Data Safety Recommendation:

Use Backup All regularly to download a .json snapshot of your work.
